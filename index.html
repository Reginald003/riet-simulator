Legend – what you see means: Browner water = more suspended sediment (turbidity). Green scum = algal bloom from excess nutrients. Fewer fish / fish floating belly-up = low dissolved oxygen. Dense reeds = healthy riparian vegetation filtering runoff. Brown streaks = runoff entering the river. Colour alone cannot prove water quality – clear water can still be polluted (e.g. dissolved salts), so always read the measurements.

Model assumptions and how to use

Open this file in any browser; no internet needed. Let f = 1 − 0.65·vegetation (buffer effect), r = rain factor (0.6/1/1.6). Nutrients (mg/L) = 0.5 + (12·runoff·(0.6+0.4r) + 6·waste)·f. Turbidity = 5 + (80·runoff·r + 60·waste + 30·industry)·(1 − 0.7·veg)·(0.5+0.5r) + 2·max(0, nutrients − 6). DO saturation = 14.6 − 0.35T + 0.003T²; DO = saturation·(1 − 0.5·waste − 0.1·industry) − 0.35·max(0, nutrients − 4)·(1+(T−10)/60) (algal decomposition demand). pH = 8 − 1.8·industry − 0.5·waste. TDS = (250 + 1500·industry·(1 − 0.2·veg) + 150·runoff + 100·waste)·(1.1 − 0.1r). ALI combines scores (DO 30%, pH 20%, nutrients 20%, turbidity 15%, TDS 15%) scaled down when DO is low and above 30 °C. Values are simplified for teaching.
